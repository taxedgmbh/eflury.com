Shortcuts
  • Phone: swipe the preview left for next, right for back.
  • Keyboard or presenter clicker: → or Space next, ← back.
  • B hide or resume slides, 1–9 jump to a slide.
  • W window view (fills this browser window only), F full screen, Esc back to the remote.
