I studied Economics-Finance at the University of Aberdeen, with an exchange year in Wisconsin. Then I spent nine years in finance inside a global corporation: controlling, forecasting and transfer pricing first, then automating finance processes.

## What automation taught me

Automating a process forces you to understand it completely. Every manual step, every exception, every "we have always done it this way". That is where I learned to think in systems, not hours.

## Why my own companies

In 2021 I founded Taxed GmbH, and in 2026 Skopa AG. Alongside them, EF Real Estate holds residential property. Finance, tax and technology meet in all three.

## What these notes are for

I will write here about what I learn while building: what works, what does not, and what I would do differently.
